PV & battery calculator
Method 1 · PV model – PVGIS time series 2020–2023 train a gradient boosting regressor (irradiance, sun position, temperature → power).
2 · Weather – MeteoSwiss ICON-CH1/CH2 forecasts; billing with SwissMetNet measurements.
3 · Statistics – load from standard load profile H0, prices as published or mean of the same quarter hour over the last 14 days.
4 · MPC – MILP every 15 min over 24 h following Ruddick et al. (2024), section 3.5.3. Price analysis
